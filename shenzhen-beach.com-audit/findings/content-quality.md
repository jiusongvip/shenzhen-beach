### Experience (20/25) — Strong

Explicit claim: "Built by Shenzhen locals who have visited every beach on this page multiple times across all seasons." Real photos, specific anecdotes ("The sand becomes a mosaic of umbrellas"), practical advice from experience ("Arrive before 8 AM on summer weekends").

**Missing:** Named author bios, photo timestamps showing seasonal variety.

### Expertise (18/25) — Good

Cites Shenzhen Ecology Bureau monthly reports. Specific water quality grades, exact bus numbers (103, 387, M191), precise facility pricing (showers 10 RMB, lockers 20 RMB), bilingual accuracy of Chinese names.

**Missing:** Individual credentials, URLs to cited Ecology Bureau reports, source for visitor count claims.

### Authoritativeness (10/25) — Developing

Schema markup is strong (TouristDestination, TouristAttraction, Person). Clear brand identity. But: site is not yet deployed, has zero external backlinks, not cited by other sources, no social proof.

### Trustworthiness (20/25) — Good

Transparent methodology: "All information verified August 2026." Explicit disclaimer: "Not affiliated with any tourism board." Consistent dates. Weekly update claim in footer.

**Missing:** No Contact page, no Privacy Policy, no Terms of Service — significant trust gap.
